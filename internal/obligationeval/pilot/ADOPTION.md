# Parent adoption for rubric calibration

The parent inspected the requests, proposed rubric, original gold and both
corrections before candidate development or execution. Adopt the proposed v2
corrections as `obligations-v1` in a separate effective file; retain all original
authoring files. D02's conditions are independently violable necessary conditions,
not a claim of sufficiency. H03's hold exception does not authorize inventing an
immediate deletion deadline after the hold lifts.

Keep the minimal candidate schema. Meaning and provisional status must remain
explicit in item text; questions may state an unresolved decision. Do not add
status/provenance/link fields before the pilot demonstrates a need for them.

Use the metric vector, not a composite score. Full semantic coverage from a
copied request can coexist with failed extraction usability; do not lower its
semantic coverage merely to force a desired ranking. Likewise, duplicates cannot
inflate recall, obligation/constraint labels are interchangeable for this set,
and source quotation is not semantic entailment.

The proposed conservative case pass is a diagnostic pilot signal, not a release
reliability claim. Keep the declared 40-item/100-claim review budget as an explicit
`needs_adjudication` boundary, never truncation or a quality judgment. Empty
denominators are N/A. Incomplete/disputed reviews do not produce a pass or fail.

Run two independent reviews of calibration responses without exposing the
author's expected reviews. Compare actual disagreements before accepting the
rubric for candidate evaluation. Both reviewers must be independent of candidate
development; every held-out output gets two reviews, with unresolved disputes
remaining unscorable. Preserve all raw judgments and any adjudication.

Withhold all H requests and all private gold/judgments from candidate development.
The candidate developer may see D requests and the public output/quality contract.
Freeze the candidate before one held-out run; later changes against these held-out
cases are regression evidence, not another independent holdout. No candidate has
been run at this adoption point.
