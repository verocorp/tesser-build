# Proposed adjudication contract — not frozen

This is an extraction evaluation, not an architecture evaluation. Judge whether an output preserves the request's meaning and uncertainty in usable items. Do not reward a favorite phrasing, preferred implementation, extra requirements, or exact agreement with a reference sentence. The candidate receives only a case ID and its request, never the gold or judgments. This document is review/scoring design, not a candidate prompt.

## Separation of responsibilities

An independent reviewer performs semantic judgment. Deterministic tooling checks JSON shape, IDs, enum values, quote membership, referential integrity, completeness of the review, and arithmetic on recorded judgments. It cannot infer entailment from substring matches, overlapping tokens, embeddings, or a candidate repeating a source quote. A matching quote can ground an invented assertion just as easily as a valid one.

Use the proposed candidate shape `{case_id, items:[{id,kind,text,evidence:[verbatim source quotes]}]}`. IDs must be unique within the case and text and evidence quotes nonempty. Kinds are goal, obligation, constraint, assumption, question. An empty items list is valid JSON with zero coverage, not a successful extraction. Do not silently repair malformed output. Format validity, quote validity, semantic quality, and extraction usability are separate results. No extra candidate fields are required yet.

## Bounded review procedure

1. Read the source and the candidate before the private gold. List each candidate item's materially distinct assertions, not each grammatical clause. Then consult the reference. The reviewer is independent of the candidate and must not rewrite it. Maximums proposed for this short-request set: 40 items per case and 100 claim fragments per judgment. Exceeding a limit is `needs_adjudication`, never silent truncation. These resource limits need parent approval.
2. Map every reference ID to zero or more candidate item IDs. Record `full`, `partial`, or `missing`, with a short semantic rationale and source quotes. Preserve actor, scope, negation, numerical comparator, timing origin, modality, exceptions, and conditions. All must be right for `full`. A conjunction in the condition remains attached to its behavior. Cross-item context is allowed when the relation is explicit (for example, “For those qualifying requests”), not when the reviewer has to invent the connection.
3. Review every candidate claim, including extra clauses inside otherwise correct items. Record `supported`, `unsupported`, `contradicted`, `explicit_proposal`, or `open_question`; cite the exact candidate fragment and the source quote that supports the claim, conflicts with it, or leaves the issue open. An absence-based judgment must say what is absent and how it is a new requirement. The presence of a verbatim quote is not a defense. A partially supported item can contain both a supported claim and an unsupported clause.
4. Flag any duplicate assertion, recording the later claim and the original. A more detailed restatement that adds a missing condition is not a complete duplicate; split only the repeated assertion where useful. Map references once when counting recall regardless of how many candidate items repeat them. Several references can map to one ordinary item, and several items can jointly cover a reference. A compact item with a refusal and its explanatory message is acceptable. Do not force candidate granularity to match gold granularity.
5. Judge semantic role separately. Obligation versus constraint is interchangeable throughout this set. Goals must preserve the actor's intended outcome, not invent a metric. Source assumptions must remain explicitly provisional: kind `assumption` plus text preserving any pending confirmation is sufficient. Conflicting commitments can be quoted within a genuine question and still earn commitment coverage. A question stating both sides must not be penalized just because its kind is `question`. Other label mismatches are not automatically missing semantics: record the mismatch and whether it materially changes the claim's status.
6. Judge every required U reference as `preserved`, `lost`, or `resolved_without_authority`. Preserve both conflicting commitments and the need for a decision; do not count an invented compromise as the decision. Unknown details can be a question or an explicit statement that they remain undecided in a question item. A provisional source assumption pending confirmation may satisfy both its A reference and its U reference. Optional tentative ideas need not be extracted, but if included cannot become commitments. Record an unsupported promotion even for an optional idea.
7. Check extraction usability. An item that copies the entire request and mixes goals, commitments, tentative ideas, and unresolved questions is `omnibus`. It may earn literal semantic coverage, but it is not a usable extraction, and fails the proposed usability gate. This is not a ban on quoting or on compound items. Split out only meaningfully different roles and independently usable behaviors; a requirement and its condition belong together. A source sentence copied as a correctly classified individual requirement is perfectly valid.
8. Record uncertainties and authoring defects, rather than bending the reference or treating all disagreement as candidate error. A second independent reviewer resolves any disputed full/partial mapping, unsupported-claim flag, conflict resolution, or omnibus flag. The second review has a maximum of one pass; unresolved cases remain `needs_adjudication` and receive no pass/fail decision. Preserve both judgments and the resolver's reasoning. Also second-review every held-out case and the weak fixtures during calibration. Do not use held-out disputes to silently tune the candidate.

## Meaning-sensitive decisions

- A missing condition makes a broader commitment than the source. Award no full reference credit and flag the unsupported broadening; do not merely count it as an omission. “Send another notice every 15 minutes” loses “while the same incident remains unhealthy.”
- `At most 10,000` includes 10,000. `At least 24 elapsed hours` includes exactly 24 and is not a calendar-day cutoff. A wrong comparator is a contradiction, not a stylistic preference. Boundary behavior should appear in either the item or its explicit referenced context; source evidence alone is not a replacement for the item's claim.
- An explicit exclusion is a commitment about scope, not a suggestion. “Annual subscriptions are out of this feature” does not mean “annual subscriptions can never be refunded.”
- A reasonable additional question is allowed if the source genuinely leaves the issue open and the question is relevant to the behavior. It need not appear in gold. Do not reward extra questions in recall, or penalize them just for being extra. A question with a false presupposition (“Which mandatory SMS vendor…?”) contains an unsupported assertion.
- A candidate-created assumption is permitted only when explicitly labeled as an unapproved proposal, does not contradict the request, and is anchored to a real open issue. It earns no commitment or source-assumption coverage. Downstream commitments built on it as though approved are unsupported. Prefer preserving the open question; do not award bonus points for speculation.
- Do not infer implementation choices, classes, APIs, delivery providers, persistence mechanisms, security policies, or performance targets absent from the request. A specific proposed implementation is unnecessary here even if reasonable.
- An uncertainty does not license dropping both sides of a conflict. A candidate that says only “clarify invoice naming” lacks both concrete rules. Conversely, preserving both rules as plain requirements with no conflict notice loses the ambiguity.

## Proposed reviewer record

The review envelope has `case_id`, `candidate_label`, `gold_version`, `review_status` (`complete` or `needs_adjudication`), `reference_judgments`, `claim_judgments`, `ambiguity_judgments`, `item_flags`, and `disagreements`. Weak fixtures demonstrate these fields. A reference row has `reference_id`, `item_ids`, `coverage`, `rationale`, and `source_quotes`. A claim row has a reviewer-assigned unique `claim_id`, `item_id`, `candidate_fragment`, `verdict`, `duplicate_of` (claim ID or null), `rationale`, and `source_quotes`. An ambiguity row has `reference_id`, `item_ids`, `status`, `rationale`, and `source_quotes`. An item flag has `item_id`, `flag`, `rationale`, and `source_quotes`.

Claims must collectively account for every material assertion in every nonempty item. A claim judgment citing source text but not explaining entailment is incomplete. Every gold reference must have exactly one reference judgment, including optional U references; every required U reference must also have one ambiguity judgment. An item ID must exist in the candidate; a reference ID must belong to the case; duplicate links must point to an earlier claim in the same judgment. `missing` references normally have empty item IDs; a `partial` or `full` reference needs at least one ID. Empty candidates still need complete reference and ambiguity judgments. These are deterministic validation rules after human/independent semantic review, not a judging algorithm.

## Metrics, with no hidden semantic matching

Report per case, and separately by split. Always publish denominators and raw issue counts.

- **Commitment recall:** fully covered O references / all O references. Partial gets zero in the primary metric and is reported separately; do not invent fractional weights. Compute micro recall for diagnostic counts and macro per-case recall so longer requests do not silently dominate.
- **Goal coverage:** fully covered G references / all G references. Report separately from commitments.
- **Source-assumption coverage:** fully covered A references / all A references. Cases with no A references are N/A, not perfect by default.
- **Required ambiguity preservation:** U references marked preserved / required U references. Optional tentative-idea omissions are not in the denominator. Report any optional idea promoted to commitment as an unsupported claim.
- **Assertion precision:** nonduplicate `supported` claim fragments / all nonduplicate asserted claim fragments (`supported`, `unsupported`, `contradicted`). `explicit_proposal` and `open_question` are excluded; embedded assertions in them are not. Empty denominator is N/A, never 1.0. This measure depends on reviewer segmentation; it is diagnostic and not the sole ranking objective.
- **Duplicates:** count of claim rows with `duplicate_of` set, plus a rate over all claim rows. Duplicates cannot increase recall or precision. Report unsupported and contradicted claim counts, material role-error counts, and omnibus item counts separately.
- **Grounding:** valid exact source quotes / all candidate evidence quotes, plus items with no valid quote. Quote validity is necessary but insufficient. Do not combine it with semantic precision or use it as a substitute.

Proposed conservative case pass: valid candidate and complete adjudication, all O/G/A references fully covered, every required U preserved, zero unsupported or contradicted assertions, zero material role errors, zero omnibus items, and at least one valid supporting source quote per item. Harmless duplication is reported but does not alone fail. No single aggregate score and no passing score for an empty candidate. This strict readiness bar is a proposal for the parent, not an empirical production-reliability claim. Ten authored cases cannot establish reliability; report 0–10 passing cases and individual failures rather than a confidence claim.

## Versioning and disputes

`private/GOLD.json` is the untouched first authoring pass. Its discovered mistakes are retained, not hidden. `private/GOLD_PATCH_PROPOSAL.json` supplies proposed corrected reference operations; the parent must decide whether to adopt them before freezing the eval. Weak fixtures use unchanged D01 references so they do not depend on that decision. Bind later judgments to a specific gold version and dataset hash. Preserve original candidate outputs and reviewer judgments, including bad judgments, in an append-only run record when the parent runs an evaluation. This authoring task does not run one.
